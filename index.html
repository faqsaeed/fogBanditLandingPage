COMMON QUESTIONS

The questions a jeweller should ask before installing security fog.

Every store should be assessed for layout, escape routes, alarm configuration and appropriate fog coverage before installation.

Will the fog damage jewellery or showcases?

Bandit UK states that its security fog is harmless and leaves no residue, and lists jewellery shops with precious metals and stones among the environments where it has been used.

Can it protect us during opening hours?

Yes. Jewellery applications can be configured for controlled daytime activation via dedicated panic buttons, alongside separate overnight burglary protection.

Does it replace our alarm or CCTV?

No. The strongest setup is layered. Your existing systems detect, record and report; Fog Bandit adds an active intervention layer once the configured trigger conditions are met.

How do we know which unit we need?

Coverage depends on store volume, layout and risk areas. Fog Bandit ANZ offers different systems and mounting options, including 320-series configurations for rooms roughly 40–200m³.

What happens after deployment?

The correct response procedure depends on your alarm and site plan. Installation and operating procedures should be agreed during the security assessment and staff training.
