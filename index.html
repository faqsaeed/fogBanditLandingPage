BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Four questions jewellers usually ask.

Everything else can be covered during the store assessment.

Will the fog damage jewellery or displays?

Fog Bandit materials state that its security fog is harmless and leaves no residue. Store-specific suitability should still be confirmed during the assessment.

Can it work with my existing alarm?

Yes. Fog Bandit is designed to operate as an active layer alongside existing intrusion and panic-alarm systems, with the exact trigger logic configured during installation.

How much coverage does my store need?

That depends on room volume, layout and the assets at risk. The 320 series includes configurations for approximately 40–200m³ spaces.

Can the same system protect us during the day and at night?

Yes. Jewellery applications can be configured for panic activation during trading hours and intrusion-triggered protection after closing.
